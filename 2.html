Description

Our best-loved candle opens with juicy fig and settles into dry cedarwood and amber. Each vessel is glazed by hand, so no two are exactly alike. Made with a cotton wick and a blend of soy and coconut wax for a slow, even burn.

Burn guide
  • Trim the wick to 5 mm before every burn.
  • On the first burn, let the wax melt to the edge (about 3 hours).
  • Never burn for more than 4 hours at a time.
Ingredients

Soy wax, coconut wax, cotton wick, fragrance oils (phthalate-free), hand-glazed stoneware vessel.
